Your questions, answered.

Is there really no downtime?

EMFACE®, Botox® and your IVs fit around a normal day. RF microneedling and Clear RF usually leave a day or two of pinkness, so we plan those sessions around your calendar.

Does it hurt?

Most people find it very manageable. EMFACE® feels like warmth and gentle muscle contractions. Botox® is a few quick injections. Before your RF microneedling and Clear RF sessions, your provider talks you through what to expect and how we keep you comfortable.

When will I see results, and how long do they last?

Changes build as the program moves through Lift and Renew, and your Reveal visit in week 12 brings after photos with your provider. How long results last varies from person to person, so you leave with a maintenance plan made for you.

Can I do Botox® and EMFACE® in the same program?

Yes. They do different jobs, which is why both are part of the plan. Your provider sets the timing between them at your consult.

How much does it cost?

There is one program price, shared at your free consult once your provider understands your goals. Payment plans are available. The IV add-ons are priced separately: Beauty Glow IV is $199 and Hydration IV is $125.

Do I have to commit to all 12 weeks?

The program is built as one 12-week plan, because each phase builds on the last. If your schedule is tight, tell us at your consult and we will plan your sessions around it.
